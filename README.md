# Shaking head AI: how the trend video works

Notes on the TikTok shaking head trend, and how to make your own version with a pet.

**Make one from a photo:** [shakinghead.apprundown.com](https://shakinghead.apprundown.com/)

This repo holds notes only. There's no code to run and no media.

## What the trend is

A short vertical clip of an animal looking far too pleased with itself. It started with a calf in October 2026 and spread to cats and dogs:

1. The eyes squeeze shut in a smug little smile, and the head dips toward the camera.
2. The head sways from side to side.
3. The eyes pop open and the animal grins straight at you.

The joke is usually in the caption, about someone acting innocent.

## Making it with AI

The clip needs three things to work:

- **A motion reference.** A depth video of the head movement keeps the timing of the sway. It carries motion only, so the pet's look comes from the photo.
- **A clear photo.** One animal, head and body in frame, both eyes visible, in good light.
- **Timed expressions.** A depth video can't show a face, so the prompt has to describe each smile and when it happens.

The hard part is keeping the head movement large. Models tend to shrink it into a slight wobble, so the prompt has to ask for big moves.

## Which photos work best

| Works well | Works badly |
|---|---|
| Looking at or near the camera | Side-on or from behind |
| Head and body in frame | Extreme close-up, or too far away |
| Daylight | Dark or blurry |
| One pet | Group shots (crop them first) |
